# Product

This is design context derived from the current Goal and final product contracts, not a new product authority. The current capability map is `docs/current/TICKETBOX_CURRENT_PRODUCT_ATLAS.md`. Design choices below are the current construction direction, not claims about the user's permanent preferences.

## Register

product

## Users

Household members capture receipts on their phones, review and correct bills, manage money owed between people, plan upcoming spending and understand their shared finances. The Owner also operates a local Windows installation. People may be interrupted, offline, unfamiliar with accounting, or limited to a read-only role.

## Product Purpose

Make family financial records understandable and dependable, from incoming evidence to confirmed facts and useful decisions. Windows owns the server facts and attachments; the originating client owns unsent intent. Recognition and advice are suggestions, never invented financial facts. Web and Android share meaning, but need not share identical layouts.

## Brand Personality

Clear, warm, composed. A receipt and its coral side clip are a useful recognizable object, not a reason to put a character on every screen. The interface should feel like a well-kept personal tool, not an operator dashboard or a marketing website.

## Anti-references

- Demo-only screens, invented figures, fake progress and controls without a real capability.
- Green top-clip/green-hat branding, which the user rejected. Historical mascots and current skins are not visual authority.
- Dense developer diagnostics in ordinary household tasks, endless identical cards, decorative hero metrics and distracting celebrations.

## Design Principles

1. Put the next real task within reach. Reduce repeated entry, navigation and correction work.
2. Keep amount, meaning, evidence and status together. Never hide uncertainty behind friendly decoration.
3. Explain a state and provide its next action. Empty, filtered-empty, loading, offline, failed and settled are different.
4. Preserve identity, ledger, permissions, drafts, command ownership and recovery while changing presentation.
5. Use one current production system. Migrate real consumers and retire replaced assets, components and entrances.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Construction targets readable contrast, native keyboard semantics, visible focus, touch-sized controls, reduced-motion support, large-text layouts, and state meaning that does not depend on color. Chinese text and long legal amounts must remain readable at compact widths. Both light and dark appearance and custom backgrounds require actual consumer inspection. These are acceptance targets, not a claim of completed conformance.
